Player Profile

I'm Ben, a game developer based in Los Angeles who loves the part of games where design and code meet: the systems, the interfaces and the little rules that decide how a game feels to play. I work mostly in Unity and Godot, and I'm finishing my studies at the University of California, Santa Cruz in 2026, after Pasadena City College, where I made the Dean's Honor list for three semesters. Before that, I was in the robotics club at Arcadia High School.

On Solais I built the branching dialogue and the Morse code minigame. On Hellbent I make combat UI, boss fights and accessibility options such as font scaling, high-contrast modes and input remapping. For the 10-day jam game Switch-Out I designed a one-button switch between 2.5D and 2D and the puzzles built around it.

I grew up playing everything from MMORPGs and turn-based RPGs to MOBAs, shooters and rhythm games, and I try to bring what makes those feel good into my own work. I'm always looking for a team to build something with.
